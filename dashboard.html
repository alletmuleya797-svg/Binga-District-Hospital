Services & Departments

Click a department to learn more or make a referral

Outpatients (OPD)

Walk-in clinic, general consults, referrals

Daily clinics for general medicine and minor procedures. OPD opens at 08:00 weekdays.

Emergency & Casualty

24/7 emergency care

Triage and stabilisation. For life-threatening conditions call the emergency number immediately.

Maternity

Antenatal, delivery & postnatal care

Comprehensive care for mothers and newborns. Emergency obstetric services available 24/7.

Laboratory

Basic tests & sample collection

Blood tests, malaria, HIV rapid tests, and sample processing. Results collection details available on the lab page.

Radiology / Imaging

X-ray & basic imaging

Imaging services for diagnostics. Refer via the radiology request form.

Pharmacy

Dispensing & medication counseling

Pharmacy open weekdays with essential medicines. Check availability or collect prescriptions.

Patients & Visitors

Admission, visiting, fees and patient rights

Visiting Hours & Admissions

Visiting hours: 14:00–16:00 daily. Emergency admissions open 24/7 at Casualty. Please bring ID and referral if applicable.

Fees & Billing

Payments may be made at the cashier on the ground floor. We accept cash and mobile money. Exemptions available for qualifying patients.

Patient Rights & Privacy

We respect confidentiality. View our full privacy policy and patient charter on the website.
